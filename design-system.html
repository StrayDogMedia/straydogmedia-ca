Stray Dog Media

The visual foundation for the site. Driven by assets/css/tokens.css — change a token, it updates across every page. Direction: evolve the mark — keep the monochrome camera-and-paw heritage, add warmth and one signal accent. Built as the twin of strAI.ca: same conventions (bilingual toggle, dark-default theme, .eyebrow, .btn-primary, GSAP reveals), its own orange brand.

01 — Principles

How it should feel

Monochrome, then punch

Ink, paper and warm greys do the work. Orange appears rarely, and it means "look here."

Cinematic & dark

Dark by default — video and photography sit best on a deep canvas. Light theme is one tap away.

Local, not corporate

Confident and modern, but warm, bilingual and human — a community storyteller.
