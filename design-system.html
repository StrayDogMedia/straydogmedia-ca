01 — Principles

How it should feel

Monochrome, then punch

Black, white and warm paper do the heavy lifting. Orange appears rarely, and it means "look here."

Show the work

A media company earns trust with images. Layouts leave big room for photo and video; type stays out of the way.

Local, not corporate

Confident and modern, but warm and human — this is a community storyteller, not a faceless agency.
