01 — Principles

How it should feel

Black is the canvas

Film black by default, in both themes for hero moments. Video, photography and the dog all sit best in the dark.

Red is the bite

One red, used rarely and never as decoration. When it appears — a slab, a band, a rule — it means look here.

Cut, not rounded

Corners are 2–4px, motion is fast, nothing floats. Sharp edges read as danger; using them consistently is what keeps it refined.

Grain over everything

A fixed noise layer and a vignette stop the black from reading as flat CSS black. Same trick the brand art uses.

Loud once, quiet after

One red band and one slab per page, maximum. The restraint everywhere else is what makes the bark land.

Local, not corporate

Hard-edged and confident, but bilingual and human — a community storyteller with teeth.

Semantic tokens (--bg, --surface, --text, --accent…) map onto these and flip automatically between light and dark. Components only reference semantic tokens. Red pairs deliberately against strAI's cyan — the two sites read as a matched set, opposite ends of the wheel.

Contrast rules

  • Blood Red is a fill colour. White on it clears 5:1 — safe for buttons, slabs and bands.
  • Hot Red is red-as-text on black (5:1). Never use Blood Red for body-size text on black.
  • Deep Red (--sdm-red-ink) is red-as-text on bone (7:1). The light theme swaps to it automatically.

Radius & elevation

Radii run 1 / 2 / 3 / 4px — near-square. The pill radius survives for chips and toggles only. On black, depth comes from a red edge-glow (--glow-red), not soft grey shadow.
